Getting a good result

  • 30–80 photos, orbiting the object with roughly 70% overlap between shots.
  • Shoot two or three passes at different heights; keep the object still and move yourself.
  • Matte surfaces and soft, even light. Gloss, chrome and black defeat photogrammetry.
  • Include a ruler or known-size object in frame — photos carry no depth data, so real-world scale has to come from a reference.
